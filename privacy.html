Accessibility statement

We want everyone, including people with disabilities, to be able to use this website. We aim to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1, level AA.

  • Full keyboard navigation and a “Skip to main content” link
  • Labeled form fields and visible focus indicators
  • Text and color contrast designed for readability
  • An accessibility panel (the blue button at the bottom left) to enlarge text, turn on high contrast, underline links, use a more readable font and stop animations
  • Portuguese pages include VLibras, which translates content into Brazilian Sign Language (Libras)

If you find a barrier on this site, please tell us at [EMAIL] or on WhatsApp and we will help you and fix it.
